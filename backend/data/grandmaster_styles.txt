Mikhail Tal

Tal embraced tactical complexity, intuitive sacrifices, and dynamic imbalance, often valuing initiative above material. He excelled at creating positions where practical defensive decisions became extraordinarily difficult, generating psychological pressure through constant tactical threats.

José Raúl Capablanca

Capablanca favoured harmonious piece coordination, minimal weaknesses, and technically flawless endgame conversion. His style emphasised simplicity, accurate calculation, and effortless exploitation of small positional advantages without unnecessary complications.

Anatoly Karpov

Karpov specialised in prophylaxis, restricting the opponent's active plans before gradually improving his own position. He accumulated microscopic positional advantages through superior piece placement, exploiting weak squares, pawn weaknesses, and passive enemy pieces until resistance collapsed.

Garry Kasparov

Kasparov combined exhaustive opening preparation with aggressive initiative and dynamic piece activity. He constantly sought to seize the initiative, using tactical energy and deep calculation to transform small advantages into overwhelming attacks.

Magnus Carlsen

Carlsen excels at maintaining pressure in balanced positions, extracting victories from seemingly equal endgames through relentless accuracy and superior practical technique. His flexible opening repertoire and exceptional understanding of piece coordination allow him to outplay opponents across a wide range of structures.

Bobby Fischer

Fischer combined classical opening principles with extraordinary calculation, tactical precision, and uncompromising competitive intensity. He consistently sought objectively strongest moves while applying continuous pressure until positional or tactical weaknesses inevitably appeared.

Tigran Petrosian

Petrosian built his play around preventive strategy, defensive resilience, and remarkable positional foresight. He frequently employed exchange sacrifices for long-term compensation, eliminating enemy initiative while strengthening control over critical squares and strategic complexes.
